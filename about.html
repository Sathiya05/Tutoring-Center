Our Story

Helping Students Succeed Since 2010

TutorCenter was founded by a group of passionate educators who saw a gap in traditional tutoring. We believe every child deserves personalized attention tailored to their unique learning style — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

What started as small after-school sessions in a local library has grown into a thriving learning center serving over 500 students each year. Our growth comes from one thing: results. When students gain confidence, grades follow.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

From a library study group to a thriving learning center — here's how we got here.

2010

The Beginning

TutorCenter starts as a small after-school study group meeting in a local library, serving a handful of neighborhood students.

2014

A Home of Our Own

We move into our first dedicated learning center with four classrooms, welcoming our first cohort of full-time tutors.

2018

Going Online

We launch live online tutoring, bringing personalized instruction to families far beyond our home city.

2021

Growing Fast

We pass 500 students a year and grow our team to more than 50 certified tutors across every subject area.

2024

Learning Anywhere

We add a free video library, downloadable study guides, and intensive exam boot camps to support every kind of learner.
