Quick Tips

Study Strategies That Actually Work

Simple, research-backed techniques you can start using at home today.

Active Recall

Quiz yourself instead of re-reading. Retrieving information strengthens memory far more than reviewing it.

Spaced Practice

Review material in short sessions across several days rather than cramming the night before an exam.

Focused Sprints

Work in 25-minute focused blocks with short breaks in between — a proven way to beat procrastination.

Teach It Back

Have your child explain the concept out loud. Teaching is the fastest way to reveal and fix gaps.
