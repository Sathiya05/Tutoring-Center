Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

What age groups do you tutor?
We tutor students from Kindergarten through 12th grade. Our programs are designed to meet the developmental needs of each age group, from foundational reading in elementary school to advanced calculus and SAT prep for high schoolers.
How do you match tutors with students?
We consider your child's subject needs, learning style, personality, and schedule. Our goal is to find a tutor who not only has expertise in the subject but also connects well with your child to build trust and engagement.
Can I switch tutors if it's not a good fit?
Absolutely. We want to ensure the best match for your child. If the chemistry isn't right or the teaching style doesn't align, we'll find a new tutor at no extra cost.
Do you offer online tutoring?
Yes! We offer both in-person and live online tutoring sessions. Our online platform includes a virtual whiteboard, screen sharing, and recording capabilities so sessions can be reviewed later.
What is the cancellation policy?
We require 24 hours notice for cancellations to avoid a late fee. However, we understand emergencies happen — contact us and we'll work with you. There's no long-term contract required for any of our plans.
